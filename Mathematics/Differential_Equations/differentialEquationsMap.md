# Differential Equations Map

## Topics/Skills

**Topics:** Numerical and functional equations, ordinary and partial differential equations, order, linearity, and solution families

**Skills:** Classify equations and check a proposed solution by differentiating it and substituting it back into the equation.

## Main Ideas

A differential equation relates an unknown function to its derivatives. Classifying it by type, order, and linearity helps describe the equation and the solutions it can have.
